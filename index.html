HOW TO PLAY

Move: use the left joystick or WASD / arrow keys.

Aim & shoot: tap FIRE on mobile; click/tap the arena to aim. On computer, aim with mouse and hold left-click to shoot.

Reload: tap RELOAD or press R. Weapon: tap WEAPON or press Q.

Survive: defeat rival bots and keep an eye on the shrinking safe zone. Outside the zone, your health drains.

This is an original offline prototype, not Free Fire and not connected to online matchmaking.
